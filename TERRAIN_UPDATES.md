# Terrain Update/Overhaul Ideas
Our world shouldn't have "biomes" in the minecraft sense, they should be a 2 layered system.

- Layer 1: the terrain shape. The terrain shape should be applied to the world to change the sphere into an interesting and navigable globe.
- Layer 2: the biome on top. Once terrain is present, we should add biomes on top of the existing shape, that way we can create unique scenarios. Similarly, we would have limitations on which biomes would go where. For now, let's just focus on terrain shape.

## Terrain Shape

I want to create 4-5 unique terrain types in my world. The types are as follows:

Original terrain: 
1. Flat region- what we currently have, just an area of flatness.
2. Hilly region- slightly more hilly areas on the sphere, creating hills and dips.
3. Mountainous region- more dramatic than the hilly region, these are mountain chains and peaks.

Unique terrain:
4. Sky islands- seemingly a chunk of the planet shattering and floating into the sky,  this area consists of a deep chasm with large floating islands + small gaps between islands toward the edge. then gradually we see smaller and more dispersed islands and island clusters near the middle and at the top of the islands.
5. Caves and Caverns- deep underground areas that flow together from the surface in any area and connect into large cavernous areas.

This is all for now, we can work on others after these are finished. Start with the original terrain and once we get that nailed down and to my liking, we can add the unique terrain.

Keep in mind that topsoil should only be so thick. For any raised terrain, it should be the norm to have raw nodes raised beneath them as well.

Additional note: please keep this design whimsical, fantastical, and fun, yet accurate. We have to add another layer with the biomes so develop with that in mind, yet we don't want to kill for realism here. That is what kills these types of games.
